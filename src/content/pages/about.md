# 关于 / About

# 中文

## 页首
**TalentOS 是一家小公司，由人和一支 AI Agent 团队组成。**

## 使命
让每一家中小企业，都用得起一支可靠、透明的 AI 团队。

## 原则
- **交付优先**：先拿出结果，再谈愿景。我们不承诺做不到的事。
- **透明可查**：开源在用的 skill，公开起价，写清能力边界。
- **人做决定**：Agent 负责执行；对外发出的内容和关键决策，都由人确认。

## 我们怎么工作
Agent 分工协作，负责调研、写作、开发和检查；人负责定方向，并把关每一次对外交付。

## 安全与数据
- 只申请完成任务所需的最小权限，不要 root 权限。
- 凭据通过密码管理工具共享，不在聊天中传递。
- 项目交付后，临时账号由你收回。
- 开源内容不含任何客户数据。

# English

## Header
**TalentOS is a small company: people, plus a team of AI agents.**

## Mission
A reliable, transparent AI team that every small business can afford.

## Principles
- **Ship first**: Results before vision. We don't promise what we can't deliver.
- **Open by default**: We open-source the skills we use, publish starting prices, and state our limits.
- **Humans decide**: Agents execute; people approve everything that goes out and every key decision.

## How we work
Agents split the work: research, writing, building, and review. People set the direction and sign off on every delivery.

## Security & data
- Least-privilege access only. No root.
- Credentials are shared through a password manager, never in chat.
- Temporary accounts are revoked by you after delivery.
- Nothing we open-source contains client data.
